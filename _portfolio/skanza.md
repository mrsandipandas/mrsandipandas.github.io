[Skanza](https://skanza.app) is a hobby project and iPhone app that uses the device camera and LiDAR sensor to automatically generate professional 2D floor plans. Walk through any room and get true-to-scale measurements, with export to DXF, PDF, SVG, and PNG for use in CAD tools or client sharing. Requires iPhone 12 Pro or later.
